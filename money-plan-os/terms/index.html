Money Plan OS terms

Last updated November 8, 2026 · Price, refunds and support

Price

Money Plan OS costs $39 CAD, paid once. It isn’t a subscription. The price includes a free update with the 2027 TFSA dollar limit once the Canada Revenue Agency announces it. The 2027 RRSP dollar limit is already in the app, and the FHSA limits are fixed.

What you get

  • A licence key on your order confirmation, which unlocks the app. You need internet once to enter it; after that the app works offline, and being offline never locks you out.
  • The Windows installer (Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit), to download from your purchase. Installing: Windows may say “Windows protected your PC” because AQuietDesk is a new publisher. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  • Both stay in your Polar purchases page, so you can find them again on a new computer.

Refunds

30 days, no reason needed. If Money Plan OS isn’t right for you, email discover@aquietdesk.com within 30 days of buying, with the email address you used at checkout, and you’ll get a full refund. If you were charged by mistake (for example, twice), email us at any time and it will be fixed.

Payments are processed by Polar, the seller of record, so any refund comes from Polar to your original payment method. It usually arrives within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

Not financial advice

Money Plan OS is an education and planning tool. It isn’t financial, tax or legal advice, and its numbers depend on what you enter. Contribution limits come from the Canada Revenue Agency’s published figures; check your own room in your CRA My Account before contributing. For important decisions, talk to a qualified professional.

Your data

Everything you enter is saved only on your computer. AQuietDesk never receives it. The app goes online only to check your licence key with Polar and to look for updates, and neither sends your plan. Keep your own backups too (Settings → Your data → Export backup).

Support

Email discover@aquietdesk.com. Support is provided by one person, so allow up to 3 business days for a reply. Please include your app version and what happened. Don’t send your backup file or licence key; support never needs them.

Licence

Your purchase lets you install and use Money Plan OS on your own computers. The app comes “as is”, without warranty.

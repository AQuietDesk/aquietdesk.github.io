Schedu Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026 · Applies to the Schedu desktop app and Schedu Pro, made by AQuietDesk (“we”, “us”).

Schedu is a Windows planner for classes, tasks and calendars. Everything stays on your PC unless you turn on a feature that needs to go online, and AQuietDesk never receives your planner data.

1. What stays on your PC

Your tasks, classes, grades, notes, focus sessions and settings are saved in a database on your computer, in %APPDATA%\app.schedu.desktop. We have no server that stores or receives this data, and Schedu has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

2. What leaves your PC, and only when you turn it on

3. Google user data

When you connect a Google account, Schedu asks Google for permission to:

  • See your calendars and their events (calendar.readonly), so they can appear in Schedu’s Today and Calendar views.
  • See, create, change and delete events (calendar.events), to keep Google Calendar events in step with the items you create or change in Schedu.
  • See, create, change and delete your task lists and tasks (tasks), to show your Google Tasks in Schedu and keep them in step with your Schedu tasks.

Schedu uses this access only to show your Google events and tasks inside Schedu and to keep them in step with your Schedu tasks. Events and tasks Schedu syncs are saved in its local database so they can be shown offline. The sign-in token Google issues is stored only on your PC. It’s never included in backups and never sent to AQuietDesk or anyone else. Google data is never sent to Anthropic, including during Schedu Pro syllabus import.

Schedu’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular, Google user data:

  • is used only to provide Schedu’s user-facing calendar and task features;
  • is not transferred to anyone else, except as needed to provide those features, to comply with law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to you;
  • is not used or transferred for advertising, including retargeting, personalized or interest-based ads;
  • is not sold, and is not used to determine credit-worthiness or for lending;
  • is not read by any person, unless you give explicit permission (for example, sending a screenshot for support), it’s needed for security, or the law requires it;
  • is not used to develop, improve or train generalized or non-personalized AI or machine-learning models.

To stop sharing, choose Settings → Google → Disconnect, which deletes Schedu’s stored Google sign-in from your PC, and remove Schedu at myaccount.google.com/permissions to revoke its access on Google’s side. Events and tasks already synced stay in Schedu, and in your Google account, until you delete them.

4. Schedu Pro

Everything Schedu Pro saves is stored only on your own computer: your Anthropic API key, your licence key, the rows read from a syllabus and the items you import. None of it is stored by AQuietDesk or anywhere else by Schedu, and AQuietDesk has no server that could receive it.

  • Your Anthropic API key is saved only on this PC, in Windows Credential Manager, set so it never syncs or roams to other computers. It isn’t in Schedu’s database, so it’s never in your backups. It’s sent only to Anthropic, to sign in your own requests (each import, and when you press Test), the same way a password is sent to log in. AQuietDesk never receives it.
  • Syllabus files go from your PC straight to Anthropic’s API under your own Anthropic account, and Anthropic handles them under its own terms (Anthropic’s privacy policy). Schedu doesn’t keep a copy of the file. It keeps, on your PC, the rows it read until you review them and the items you approve. Nothing from Google Calendar or Google Tasks is ever sent to Anthropic.
  • Your licence key and its activation record are saved only on this PC, the same way as the API key. Polar, which issued the key, sees the details in the table above so it can limit how many computers a key unlocks. Settings → Schedu Pro → Remove from this PC deletes them from your PC and frees the slot.
  • Installing Pro downloads the Pro version from GitHub, like any update.

5. Purchases

If you pay for Schedu or subscribe to Schedu Pro, the purchase is handled by Polar, which acts as the seller of record and processes your payment under its own privacy policy. We receive your email address and order details from Polar so we can provide support and refunds. We never receive your card details.

6. Sharing

We don’t sell or share your information. The services above receive only what’s listed, and only when you use the feature: Google (sync), Open-Meteo (weather), GitHub (updates), Polar (purchases and Pro licences) and Anthropic (Pro import, on your own key). We may disclose information if the law requires it.

7. Security and retention

Data on your PC is protected by your Windows account. Everything Schedu sends travels over encrypted HTTPS connections. Local data stays until you delete it. Order records are kept by Polar and by us as required for tax and accounting.

8. Deleting your data

  • If you used Schedu Pro, first choose Settings → Schedu Pro → Remove from this PC and remove your API key on the same page. That deletes both from Windows Credential Manager.
  • Uninstall Schedu from Settings → Apps in Windows and tick Delete the application data, or delete the %APPDATA%\app.schedu.desktop folder yourself. Delete any backup copies in the folder you chose.
  • To have your order records deleted, email us, subject to legal retention rules.

9. Children

Schedu isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. A change that sends any new data off your PC will also be listed in the release notes.

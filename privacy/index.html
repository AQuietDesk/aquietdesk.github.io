Schedu Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026 · Applies to the Schedu desktop app and Schedu Pro, made by AQuietDesk (“we”, “us”).

Schedu is a Windows planner for classes, tasks and calendars. Your planner stays on your PC. Schedu only goes online for features you turn on, and AQuietDesk never receives your planner data or your Google data. We have no server that stores it.

1. Data Schedu handles

  • Planner data: the tasks, classes, grades, notes, quick notes, focus sessions and settings you enter.
  • Google data (only if you connect Google): the Google Calendar and Google Tasks data described in section 3, and the sign-in tokens Google issues to Schedu.
  • Schedu Pro data (only if you use Pro): your licence key, your Anthropic API key, and the syllabus files you choose to import.

Schedu has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. It doesn’t ask Google for your email address or profile.

2. Where it’s stored

  • Planner data is saved in a database on your computer, in %APPDATA%\app.schedu.desktop.
  • Google sign-in tokens, your licence key and your Anthropic API key are saved only on your PC, in Windows Credential Manager, set so they never roam to other computers. They’re not in Schedu’s database, so they’re never in backups.
  • Backups (optional) are copies of the database written to a folder you choose. If that folder is synced by Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox, that service stores the copy under its own terms.

3. Google user data

Connecting Google is optional. If you connect, you sign in on Google’s own page in your browser, and Schedu asks for three permissions. Here is what each one is used for:

PermissionWhat Schedu does with it
Google Tasks
tasks		When sync is on, Schedu creates a task in your default Google Tasks list for each Schedu item, updates it when you change the item, and deletes it when you delete the item. It checks that list for changes you make in Google to those same tasks (title, notes, done, deleted) and applies them in Schedu. Your other Google Tasks are ignored: Schedu doesn’t import, show or change them.
See your calendars and events
calendar.readonly		Schedu lists your calendars so you can choose which ones to show, and reads their events to display them in its Today and Calendar views. Events are read when the view is shown. Schedu doesn’t save or change them.
Edit events
calendar.events		Some Schedu items are linked to an event in your primary calendar (created by an earlier version of Schedu). For those items only, Schedu updates the event when you change the item, deletes it when you delete the item, and applies changes you make to that event in Google. Schedu doesn’t create new events, and doesn’t change any other event.

What Schedu keeps: for each synced item, the ID of its Google task or event, and any edits you made in Google that were applied to the item. These are saved with the item in the local database. Events from your calendars that Schedu only displays aren’t saved.

Where Google data goes: only between your PC and Google, over HTTPS. It’s never sent to AQuietDesk. Google user data is never sent to Anthropic or any AI service, including during Schedu Pro syllabus import.

Schedu’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular, Google user data:

  • is used only to provide Schedu’s user-facing calendar and task features described above;
  • is not transferred to anyone else, except as needed to provide or improve those features, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets with notice to you;
  • is not used or transferred for serving advertisements, including retargeting, personalized or interest-based ads;
  • is not sold;
  • is not read by any person, unless you give explicit permission (for example, sending a screenshot for support), it’s needed for security purposes such as investigating abuse, or the law requires it;
  • is not used or transferred to determine credit-worthiness or for lending purposes;
  • is not used to develop, improve or train generalized or non-personalized AI or machine-learning models.

Disconnecting Google

Choose Settings → Google → Disconnect in Schedu. That revokes Schedu’s access at Google and deletes the Google tokens from your PC. You can also remove Schedu at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After you disconnect, items you synced stay in Schedu until you delete them (or delete the app’s data folder, below), and tasks or events Schedu created in your Google account stay there until you delete them in Google.

5. Schedu Pro syllabus import

The syllabus file goes from your PC straight to Anthropic’s API under your own Anthropic account, and Anthropic handles it under its own terms. Schedu doesn’t keep a copy of the file. It keeps, on your PC, the rows it read until you review them, and the items you approve. Checking for items already in the class happens on your PC after Anthropic replies; those items are never sent. Your API key is sent only to Anthropic, to sign your own requests. AQuietDesk never receives it.

6. Purchases

Payments for Schedu and Schedu Pro are handled by Polar, the seller of record, which processes your payment and issues licence keys under its own privacy policy. We receive your email address and order details from Polar so we can give support and refunds. We never receive your card details.

7. Sharing, sale and advertising

We don’t sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone beyond the services in section 4, which receive only what is listed there. We don’t use your data, including Google data, to train AI models. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

8. Security

Everything Schedu sends travels over encrypted HTTPS connections. Data on your PC is protected by your Windows account, and secrets are kept in Windows Credential Manager rather than in the database.

9. Retention and deleting your data

Data on your PC stays until you delete it. We hold no copy of your planner or Google data, so there’s nothing for us to delete on our side.

  • Google: Settings → Google → Disconnect (see section 3), and optionally remove Schedu at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Schedu Pro: Settings → Schedu Pro → Remove from this PC, and remove your API key on the same page. That deletes both from Windows Credential Manager.
  • Everything else: uninstall Schedu from Windows Settings → Apps and tick Delete the application data, or delete the %APPDATA%\app.schedu.desktop folder. Delete any backup copies in the folder you chose.
  • Order records are kept by Polar and by us as required for tax and accounting. Email us to have yours deleted, subject to those legal rules.

10. Children

Schedu isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, email us and we’ll delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. A change that sends any new data off your PC will also be listed in the app’s release notes before it ships.

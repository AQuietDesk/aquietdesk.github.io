Schedu Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026 · Applies to the Schedu desktop app and Schedu Pro, made by AQuietDesk (“we”, “us”).

Schedu is a desktop planner for classes, tasks and calendars. It is built to keep your information on your own computer. This policy explains what information Schedu handles, why, where it goes, and the choices you have.

1. Summary

  • Your planner data (tasks, classes, notes, settings) is stored locally on your computer. We do not run servers that store it.
  • Google Calendar and Google Tasks sync is optional. If you turn it on, Schedu accesses your Google Calendar and Google Tasks only to show them in the app and keep them in sync with your Schedu items.
  • We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use Google user data to train AI models.
  • You can disconnect Google and delete all local data at any time.

2. Information Schedu handles

Data you create in the app

Tasks, classes, assignments, events, notes, timer sessions and preferences you enter. This is saved in files on your computer and is not sent to us.

Google user data (only if you connect Google)

When you choose to connect Google, you sign in through Google and grant Schedu the following permissions:

  • View your calendars (calendar.readonly): your list of calendars and the events in them (titles, times, locations, descriptions), so they can appear in Schedu’s Today and Calendar views.
  • View and edit events (calendar.events): to create, update and delete Google Calendar events that correspond to items you create or change in Schedu.
  • Create, edit and delete Google Tasks (tasks): to show your Google Tasks in Schedu and keep them in sync with tasks you manage in Schedu.

Events and tasks that Schedu syncs are saved in Schedu’s local database on your computer so they can be shown offline. Google sign-in tokens are also stored in that local database so Schedu can stay connected. None of this is sent to us.

Purchases and licence keys

Payments for Schedu and Schedu Pro are processed by our merchant of record, Polar. Polar receives your name, email and payment details to complete the purchase; we receive order details (such as your email and what you bought) but never your full card number. Schedu Pro checks your licence key with Polar to unlock Pro features.

Schedu Pro syllabus import

If you use syllabus import, the syllabus file you choose is sent directly from your computer to Anthropic’s API using your own Anthropic API key, so it can be read and turned into classes and deadlines. This happens only when you start an import. Your Google user data is not included in these requests. Anthropic’s handling of that file is covered by Anthropic’s privacy policy and your API agreement with them.

App updates

Schedu may check our public release page on GitHub for new versions. This request does not include your planner data.

3. How we use information

  • To show your tasks, classes and calendar events in one place.
  • To create, update or delete Google Calendar events and Google Tasks so they stay in sync with your Schedu items.
  • To process purchases and validate licence keys.
  • To respond if you email us for support.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or selling to anyone.

4. Google API Services: Limited Use

Schedu’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular:

  • Google user data is used only to provide and improve Schedu’s user-facing calendar and task features.
  • It is not transferred to third parties, except as needed to provide those features, to comply with law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with your notice.
  • It is not used or transferred for serving ads, including retargeting, personalized or interest-based ads.
  • It is not sold, and it is not used to determine credit-worthiness or for lending purposes.
  • No person reads it, unless you give explicit permission (for example, sending us a screenshot for support), it is needed for security purposes or to comply with law.
  • It is not used to develop, improve or train generalized or non-personalized AI or machine-learning models.

5. Sharing

We share information only with the services needed to run Schedu: Google (if you connect Google Calendar and Tasks), Polar (purchases and licences), and Anthropic (only for Pro syllabus import, using your own key). We may disclose information if required by law.

6. Storage, security and retention

Planner data and Google tokens stay on your computer, protected by your computer’s own account security. Data travelling to Google, Polar or Anthropic is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. Local data stays until you delete it or uninstall Schedu. Purchase records are kept by Polar and us as required for tax and accounting.

7. Your choices

  • Disconnect Google: use “Disconnect” in Schedu’s settings, or remove Schedu at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting in Schedu deletes its stored Google tokens from your computer; to also revoke Schedu’s access on Google’s side, remove it at the Google link above. Events and tasks already synced stay in your local planner until you delete them.
  • Delete your data: uninstall Schedu and delete its app data folder. Because we don’t hold your planner data, there is nothing on our side to delete.
  • Purchase data: email us to request access to or deletion of your order records, subject to legal retention rules.

8. Children

Schedu is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, note it in the app’s release notes.

Etyvo and your privacy

Last updated November 11, 2026

Etyvo has no account, no analytics, no ads and no server of its own. The dictionary is part of the app, and your lookups never leave your PC.

What Etyvo stores, and where

One file, etyvo.sqlite3, in %APPDATA%\app.etyvo.desktop on your PC. It holds:

  • the words you saved and the meaning chosen for each,
  • the sentence each word was found in,
  • the name of the app and the window title you were reading in (turn this off in Settings, under “Remember where a word came from”),
  • your review history, your settings, and your activated licence key.

Settings has buttons to export this, back it up, open the folder, and delete your words.

What Etyvo reads

Only when you press the lookup shortcut, Etyvo reads the text you have highlighted in the app in front, and up to about 400 characters on either side of it to find the sentence. It keeps the one sentence that holds your word and discards the rest. It never reads password boxes.

In apps that don’t share their selection, Etyvo copies it with Ctrl+C and then puts back what was on your clipboard. If you use Windows clipboard history, the copied word may appear there.

When Etyvo goes online

  • Activating and checking your licence key. When you enter your key, and at most once a month after that when you’re online, Etyvo sends Polar (the store that sold the key) your key, a one-way hash of your PC’s Windows machine ID (the ID itself never leaves the PC), your PC’s name (so you can tell your PCs apart in your purchases page) and the Etyvo version. Polar’s own privacy policy covers what it does with your order.
  • Checking for updates. Only when you press “Check for updates” in Settings. Etyvo asks GitHub whether a newer version exists, and downloads it if you choose to install.

Polar and GitHub see your IP address, as any website does. Nothing about your words, sentences or reviews is ever sent.

Buying

When you buy Etyvo, Polar handles the payment and receives your name, email and payment details. AQuietDesk sees your email and order in Polar to give you support and refunds, and doesn’t use it for anything else or send marketing.

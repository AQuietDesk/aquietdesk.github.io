Not the batter you bake with, or the one at bat: Etyvo reads the sentence and picks the meaning that fits.

What it does

Look up from anywhere

Highlight a word and press Ctrl+Shift+D in your browser, Word, Notepad, PDFs and most other apps. A small window opens beside your pointer.

Every lookup is kept

Each word goes into your list with the sentence you found it in, so you remember where you met it.

Review, timed for you

Words come back on a spaced-repetition schedule: tomorrow, in six days, then at longer gaps. A few minutes a day.

Built-in dictionary

About 118,000 meanings, with examples and similar words. Lookups work offline.

Pick a word from a sentence

Highlight a whole sentence and click the word you want.

Yours to take away

Export your words to a spreadsheet or Anki cards, or save a full backup.

Your words stay on your PC

There’s no account, and AQuietDesk never receives your words. Your list, sentences and reviews are saved in one file on your computer. Etyvo goes online for two things only, and neither sends your words:

  • Your licence key, checked with Polar once when you enter it and then at most once a month. Being offline never locks you out.
  • Updates, when you press “Check for updates” in Settings.

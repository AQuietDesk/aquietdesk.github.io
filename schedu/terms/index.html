Schedu terms

Last updated November 7, 2026 · Refunds, Schedu Pro and support

Price

Schedu is free. On Polar you can pay whatever you like for it, including nothing. Paying unlocks no extra features. It supports further work on the app.

Schedu Pro

Schedu Pro is a yearly subscription sold through Polar. It adds syllabus import to Schedu.

  • What you get: a licence key that unlocks Schedu Pro on up to 3 computers at a time. You can move it between computers with Settings → Schedu Pro → Remove from this PC.
  • See it before you buy: Import syllabus on any class page shows what Pro does before you pay. Please read this page and that preview first, because Pro isn’t refundable (below).
  • Your own Anthropic account: syllabus import uses your own Anthropic API key. Anthropic bills you directly for each import (usually a few cents), under Anthropic’s terms. That cost isn’t part of the subscription and AQuietDesk can’t refund it.
  • Your data stays on your device: everything Pro saves, including your API key and licence key, is kept only on your own computer. The privacy policy lists exactly what is sent and to whom.
  • Renewal and cancelling: the subscription renews each year until you cancel, which you can do at any time from your Polar purchases page. Cancelling stops the next renewal; Pro keeps working until the end of the year you paid for. After that, import turns off, but everything you imported stays in Schedu and the free app keeps working.
  • Offline use: Schedu Pro checks your key about once a week and keeps working for 30 days without a connection.
  • Check before you save: imported dates come from your syllabus and are flagged when they’re missing or unusual, but you’re responsible for checking them against the syllabus. The review screen shows the source text for every row.

Refunds

Schedu (free app): if you chose to pay for Schedu and want your money back, email discover@aquietdesk.com within 30 days of paying, with the email address you used at checkout. You’ll get a full refund, and you don’t have to give a reason.

Schedu Pro: all sales are final. Schedu Pro, including renewals, isn’t refundable, because the licence key unlocks everything it does as soon as it’s activated. This doesn’t limit any right to a refund that the law where you live gives you and can’t be waived. If you were charged by mistake (for example, twice for the same year), email us and it will be fixed.

Payments are processed by Polar, the seller of record, so any refund comes from Polar to your original payment method. It usually arrives within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

Support

Email discover@aquietdesk.com. Support is provided by one person, so allow up to 3 business days for a reply. It helps to include:

  • your Schedu version (shown at the bottom of Settings);
  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead;
  • a screenshot, if something looks wrong.

Please don’t send your backup file, Google password, licence key or Anthropic API key. Support never needs any of them.

How Schedu uses Google data

Google sign-in is optional. If you choose to connect Google, Schedu reads your calendars, events and Google Tasks so it can show them alongside your planner, and creates, updates or deletes events and tasks to keep them in sync with the items you manage in Schedu. This data stays on your computer and is used only to provide these features. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used to train AI models. You can disconnect at any time from Schedu's settings or from your Google account permissions.

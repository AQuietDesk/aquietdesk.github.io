Why Schedu asks for Google access

Google sign-in is optional, and Schedu works fully without it. If you connect your Google account, Schedu asks for three permissions, each for one feature:

Google Tasks

Schedu puts your planner items in your Google Tasks list and keeps them up to date, so they show on your phone and in Google Calendar. Edits you make there to those same tasks come back to Schedu. Your other Google Tasks are left alone.

See your Google Calendar

Schedu shows the events from the calendars you pick next to your tasks in its Today and Calendar views, so you can plan around them. It only reads them, and doesn’t save them.

Edit Google Calendar events

Keeps calendar events that earlier versions of Schedu linked to an item up to date, and deletes them when you delete the item. Schedu doesn’t create new events or change any other event.

Schedu never asks for your email address or profile. Your Google data goes only between your computer and Google: AQuietDesk never receives it, it’s never sent to Anthropic or any AI service, and it isn’t sold, used for ads, or used to train AI models. Settings → Google → Disconnect revokes Schedu’s access and deletes its Google sign-in from your computer. You can also remove it in your Google account permissions.
